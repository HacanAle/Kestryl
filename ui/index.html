Look up an IP address

Owner, network, location and abuse contact of any public address, from ipinfo.io and the official internet registries (RDAP). Free, no account.

How to read this
  • Paste an address from Connections, an alert or Listening ports. Every destination and alert also has an IP info button.
  • Operator is who runs the network. Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare, Akamai and Fastly host thousands of sites, so their name tells you the hosting company, not which site it is. Look at the domain in Connections for that.
  • Worth a look: an unknown or unsigned program talking to a home internet provider (a residential address), a small hosting company, or a country you have nothing to do with.
  • Abuse contact is where to report an attack coming from that address.
  • Private addresses (192.168.x.x, 10.x.x.x) are inside your own network and cannot be looked up.
