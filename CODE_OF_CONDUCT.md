# Code of conduct

Kestryl follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers and people who aren't in IT, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated. That includes personal data found in logs, screenshots or bug reports.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainers through a [private report](https://github.com/HacanAle/Kestryl/security/advisories/new) (choose it even if it isn't a security issue) or by contacting the repository owner on GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who break these rules.
